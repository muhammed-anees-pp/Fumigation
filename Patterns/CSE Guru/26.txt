Q26:

* * * * *
*       *
*       *
*       *
* * * * *

Ans:

#include <stdio.h>
int main(){
	int i,j,row=5;
	for(i=1;i<=row;i++){
		for(j=1;j<=row;j++){
			if(i==1 || i==row || j==1 || j==row){
				printf("* ");
			}
			else{
				printf("  ");
			}
		}
		printf("\n");
	}

	return 0;
}

Working:

Iteration 1 (i=1)
j=1 2 3 4 5 (* * * * *)

Iteration 2 (i=2)
j=1 2 3 4 5 (*       *)
