Q17:

* * * 3
*
* 
* * * * * * 6
*
* 
* * * * * * * * * 9	
*
*

Ans:

#include <stdio.h>
int main(){
	int row=3,i,j,k;
	for(i=1;i<=row;i++){
		for(j=1;j<=i*3;j++){
			printf("* ");
		}
		printf("\n");
		for(k=1;k<=2;k++){
			printf("*\n");
		}
	}
	
	
	return 0;
}


Working:

Iteration 1 (i=1)
j=1 2 3
k=1 2

Iteration 2 (i=2)
j=1 2 3 4 5 6
k=1 2

Iteration 3 (i=3)
j=1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9
k=1 2
