Q13:

*
* * *
* * * * *
* * * * * * *
* * * * * * * * *

Ans:

#include <stdio.h>
int main(){
	int i,j,row=5;
	for(i=1;i<=row;i++){
		for(j=1;j<=i*2-1;j++){
			printf("* ");
		}
		printf("\n");
	}	

	return 0;
}

Working:

Iteration 1 (i=1)
j=1

Iteration 2 (i=2)
j=1 2 3

Iteration 3 (i=3)
j=1 2 3 4 5

Iteration 4 (i=4)
j=1 2 3 4 5 6 7
